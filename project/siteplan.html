Discover Ghana

Website Plan - WDD 131 Final Project

Site Name

Discover Ghana

This name is short, easy to remember, and immediately communicates the site's purpose to first-time visitors: exploring Ghana's places, food, and culture. It works equally well for a diaspora audience reconnecting with the country as it does for a tourist planning a first trip.

Optional domain availability: discover-ghana.net

Site Purpose

The site serves as a starting point for anyone planning a visit to Ghana. It introduces the country, highlights notable destinations and traditional dishes through a filterable, interactive guide, lets visitors save the places and foods that interest them for later, and provides a simple way to reach out with trip-planning questions.

Scenarios

Questions a visitor from the target audience might ask when arriving at the site:

  • What are the must-see attractions in Ghana for a first-time visitor?
  • What traditional Ghanaian dishes should I try, and what are they made of?
  • How do I get in touch with someone to help plan the details of my trip?

Color Schema

The palette is drawn directly from the Ghanaian flag, reinforcing the site's subject at a glance while keeping enough contrast for readable body text.

  • Red – #ce1126

    Header and footer background, primary headings, active nav state.

  • Gold – #fcd116

    Accent color for buttons, links on hover, and highlighted UI elements.

  • Green – #006b3f

    Secondary accent for borders, dividers, and section underlines.

  • Charcoal – #222222

    Default body text color on light backgrounds.

  • Off-white – #fdf8f0

    Page background, keeping the palette from feeling stark against pure white.

Typography

Two Google Fonts, matching the pairing already established on the rest of the site:

  • Quicksand

    Used for all headings (h1–h3) and site navigation.

  • Nunito Sans

    Used for body copy, form labels, and general page content.
